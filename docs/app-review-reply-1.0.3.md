# Reply to App Review — MindKeep 1.0.3 (build 10)

Paste into App Store Connect → the rejected submission (View Submission) → reply to the App Review message.

---

Hello,

Thank you for the review. We have addressed both issues in build 10 of version 1.0.3.

**Guideline 3.1.1 – In-App Purchase**
The Plus and Pro auto-renewable subscriptions (monthly and yearly) are now offered with In-App Purchase. To find them: Sections > Account > See all plans. The screen shows the App Store prices, the subscription length, the auto-renewal terms, Restore Purchases, and links to the Terms of Use (EULA) and the Privacy Policy. The same screen opens when a free account taps a Pro feature, such as the meal photo scan in Nutrition. The four subscriptions are attached to this version for review.

The demo account is on the free plan, so a purchase can be tested with a sandbox account. A plan bought earlier on our website also unlocks the same features in the app (Guideline 3.1.3(b)). The app does not link to or encourage purchases outside the App Store.

**Guideline 1.4.1 – Safety: Physical Harm**
Every calculation and default goal in Nutrition now lists its source with a link to the original publication: tap "Sources" next to the Nutrition title, or "Sources behind this calculation" in the calorie calculator (Nutrition > Estimate my goal). Nutrition also states that MindKeep is not a medical app and recommends talking to a doctor or dietitian before changing a diet.

Sign-in details for the demo account are in App Review Information (email, password, and the fixed one-time code in the notes).

Thank you,
The MindKeep team
